Field Manual

Mission, Equipment & Weekly Structure ›
Mission: develop a strong, functional core and reveal visible abdominal definition while improving military-style bodyweight strength, endurance and total-body fitness.

Personalized seven-day battle rhythm

Dynamic calendar: the date selected in the interview becomes Program Day 1, regardless of weekday. Every calendar cell then displays its true weekday and date.
1-Year Campaign Architecture ›
Recovery is programmed, not improvised. Weeks 4 and 8 reduce working sets by approximately 40%. Week 12 tapers before the final PT test.
Performance-Gated Progression ›
  • Record actual repetitions or hold seconds and the repetitions you had left in reserve.
  • When every set reaches the top of the range with at least 2 RIR, the session earns one advancement mark.
  • Two advancement marks move that exercise to the next variation.
  • Missing the minimum range means hold or regress. Joint pain means stop that movement and use a pain-free regression.
  • Deload and taper weeks never trigger automatic advancement.
How the Personalization Interview Changes the Plan ›
  • Baseline repetitions select the initial movement variation.
  • Available equipment swaps pull-ups, dips and hanging core work for floor-based alternatives when necessary.
  • Session length controls exercise count and working sets without deleting the primary movement patterns.
  • Weekly availability changes the seven-day battle rhythm while preserving at least one protected rest day.
  • Low-impact and joint-sensitivity selections replace jumps, dips, overhead work or long-lever abdominal work where appropriate.
  • Post-workout logs determine whether each movement advances, holds or regresses.
Readiness Rules and Recovery Control ›
Green: normal soreness, no joint pain, reasonable sleep — execute the full prescription.
Yellow: poor sleep, unusual fatigue or heavy soreness — one fewer set per movement and keep at least 3 RIR.
Red: sharp pain, illness, severe fatigue or unsafe movement quality — the app substitutes walking, mobility and breathing. A modified recovery session protects the adherence streak without pretending it was a strength session.
Core Development for Visible Abs ›
  • Anti-extension: RKC planks, hollow-body work and dead bugs.
  • Posterior pelvic control: strict hanging knee raises and reverse-crunch mechanics.
  • Lateral stability: side planks and unilateral carries.
  • Bracing under movement: crawls, pull-ups, push-ups, squats and military circuits.
No spot reduction: direct abdominal work develops the muscle; calorie control, daily movement and time reduce the fat layer covering it.

Definition Nutrition Rules ›
  • Use the calculator as a starting estimate, not an absolute prescription.
  • A mild deficit of roughly 10–15% is usually more compatible with strength retention than an aggressive crash diet.
  • Distribute protein across three to five meals. The calculator uses approximately 1.8–2.2 g/kg/day during the definition phase.
  • Prioritize lean halal meats, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu, tempeh, lentils, beans and a suitable protein powder when needed.
  • Build meals around protein and vegetables; measure oils, nuts, nut butters, ghee and sauces.
  • Keep liquid calories, fried foods and frequent desserts controlled rather than relying on perfection or food bans.
  • Adjust only after two consistent weeks of weight, waist and adherence data.
Android Opening, Cache Clearing & Recovery ›
Correct opening method: save the file in the phone’s Downloads folder, then open the actual .html file with Chrome or Samsung Internet. A Drive, Files or document preview may display a blank page because it does not execute the application JavaScript.
Do not refresh a stale content address: an Android content:// link is a shared-file reference, not the permanent file location. Back out and reopen the file from Downloads.
Cache versus program data: clearing only temporary cached images is different from clearing cookies/site data or clearing the browser application’s storage. The latter can remove workout history and photos.
Minimum backup protocol: export JSON weekly and create a Portable HTML after every checkpoint. Keep those files in Downloads, OneDrive, Google Drive or another location independent of the browser.
Best long-term configuration: host the PWA package on HTTPS and install it to the home screen. The program shell can then reload from its stable web address. Local progress still requires backups unless cloud synchronization is added.
Safety & Stop Rules ›
Stop immediately for chest pain, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, sudden weakness, severe headache or sharp joint/back pain. Seek appropriate medical assessment.
Muscular effort and controlled burning are expected. Sharp pain, instability, numbness, tingling or pain that worsens each set are not acceptable training signals.
General fitness and nutrition information only. Obtain medical clearance when appropriate, particularly with cardiovascular, metabolic, orthopedic or other health concerns.

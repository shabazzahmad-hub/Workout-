**How to use:** attach the still named in the heading, paste the shared line, then the motion line. Square stills: ask for 16:9, then crop the middle square afterwards (the script does this for you).

**Shared line (paste after every motion line):**

> The same man, the same military fatigues and the same plain studio background as the attached image. One person only. Fixed camera, no zoom, no cuts, no text, no captions. Smooth, controlled, correct form at a steady pace, repeating the movement two or three times and finishing in exactly the starting position so the clip loops. Silent.

**Check every clip.** If the person does a different exercise from the one named, regenerate with a sharper motion line. A wrong clip is worse than the still.

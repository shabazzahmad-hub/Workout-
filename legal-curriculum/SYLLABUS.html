One lesson a day, about 10 minutesDelivered at 8:30 PM MountainStarted October 27, 2026

How each lesson is built

  • Plain English first. Then the professional framework. Then one worked example.
  • One beginner mistake. One checklist. One self-test. One task to apply today.
  • Round hypothetical numbers only. No project figures. No retired facts.
  • Any law, rate or treaty is cited to a primary source or tagged "unverified".
  • Every lesson names the licensed professional who must sign off before you act.
  • Every page has a Listen button that reads the lesson aloud.
